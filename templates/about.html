About StockSense

StockSense is a stock market prediction app that uses an LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory) deep learning model to forecast where a stock's price is headed over the next 7 trading days. Just type in a ticker — from NASDAQ or the NSE — and get an instant forecast, chart, and a simple Buy / Sell / Hold call.

What you can do here

  • Search any stock — enter a symbol like AAPL, TSLA, TCS, or INFY and get results in seconds.
  • See a 7-day forecast — powered primarily by an LSTM neural network trained on that stock's own recent price history.
  • Compare against other models — ARIMA and Linear Regression forecasts are shown alongside the LSTM prediction, so you can see how they agree or differ.
  • Track a live dashboard — quick snapshot cards for a handful of popular stocks, updated in real time.
  • Get in touch — questions or feedback go straight to us via the Contact page.

Why LSTM?

Stock prices are a sequence — today's price depends on yesterday's, last week's, and the broader trend before that. LSTM networks are a type of recurrent neural network built specifically to learn from sequences like this, which makes them a natural fit for price forecasting compared to models that treat each day in isolation.

The team

StockSense was built as a project applying LSTM-based deep learning to real-world stock market data, combining a Flask web app with live data from Yahoo Finance.

StockSense is for educational purposes only and is not financial advice. Markets are volatile and no model can guarantee future prices.
